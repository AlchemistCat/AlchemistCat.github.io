A cozy adventure in the making...

Elara is a little cat with an alchemist's path ahead of her. The plan is to build a relaxed 2D adventure around exploration, discovery, gardening and alchemy.

Open by design!

The goal is to keep the game free and open source, use openly licensed assets where practical, and document licenses clearly.
