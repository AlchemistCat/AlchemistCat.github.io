Let's make something lovely! 💜

"The Alchemist Cat: Adventure of Elara" is a little indie game project that's still taking shape. It's early days, and there's a lot left to imagine!

I'd love for this project to grow in the open, with help from people who enjoy cozy adventures, cats, and creative little worlds! :3

What could you help with?

  • Character art, backgrounds, and animation
  • Story ideas, writing, and worldbuilding
  • Music and sound effects
  • Godot development and programming
  • Feedback and general suggestions

These are just ideas, not a list of requirements. You don't have to be an expert, and you don't need to contribute to every part of the project.

How can you get involved?

It's really simple! Tell me a little about yourself and how you'd like to help. Examples of your work are welcome, but they're not required just to say hello! 😸
